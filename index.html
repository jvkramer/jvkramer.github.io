Assistant Professor · University of Copenhagen

John V. Kramer

I am an Assistant Professor at the Department of Economics at the University of Copenhagen.

I received my PhD from the Institute for International Studies (IIES) at Stockholm University in 2022. Prior to my time in Stockholm, I completed my undergraduate studies at Maastricht University and worked at the Bundesbank, the Ifo Institute in Munich and the Kiel Institute for the World Economy.

My research interests lie in the field of Macroeconomics and Household Finance, with a particular focus on heterogeneity.
