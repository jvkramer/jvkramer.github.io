Copenhagen → Cancún

Whether a fare is expensive is a question about its position in a distribution, not its level. This tracks the cheapest CPH–CUN return fare on sale and places it against the spread of historical fares for the same route and departure month.

Today's fare against the historical spread

Each box is the historical fare distribution for that departure month: the box spans the middle half of past fares (25th–75th percentile), the line inside is the median, the whiskers reach the cheapest and dearest fares on record. The red dot is what the same trip costs today.

The cheapest fare found on each collection date, for one departure month at a time, against that month's historical median. This fills in as the collector is re-run.
